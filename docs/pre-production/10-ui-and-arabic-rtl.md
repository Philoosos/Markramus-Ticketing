# UI pages and Arabic RTL specification

## Public pages

- Arabic-first home for the single production, fixed performances and booking entry.
- Production/performance details, venue and approved seat map with ordinary, photography and VIP distinction. Photography is visible/unavailable; VIP is explained separately and is not a seat.
- Online booking supports 1–9 tickets and repeat independent bookings. Owner name/mobile, delivery preference WhatsApp/email/both, optional email only if email selected, non-Christian attendee question/count, and secure identity-document procedure when applicable. No customer account or OTP.
- Seat checkout shows proof-upload deadline countdown (15 minutes), InstaPay instructions/proof upload, and clear pending Admin review status without a running expiry after timely upload.
- Private tracking page shows booking state and downloads issued tickets. Lost-link instruction directs customer to Booking Officer; no mobile-only lookup.
- Arabic RTL, responsive desktop/mobile, keyboard/touch/screen-reader support, branded colors only when officially approved; no fake logo.

## Staff pages

- Individual Super Admin, Admin, Booking Officer and Scanner sign-in. Customers have no account or password.
- Officer group booking from 10 tickets: 25-minute timer, pause/resume for pending discount/comp approvals, full payment, Cash confirmation or InstaPay proof review status, affiliation and organizer delivery selection.
- Super Admin/Admin prioritized proof review queue showing age/wait duration; approve/reject with reason; booking, refund, team/actor roster, VIP issuance/revocation, production changes, audit and reports.
- Scanner in mobile browser with valid/already used/cancelled/invalid states plus accessible color, sound and vibration feedback; successful scans update all devices immediately.
- Reports visible to Officers with financial data; export controls only for Admin.

## Content and pending design

Use the established palette: deep theatrical red, black, warm off-white and restrained antique gold; do not invent or use a fake logo. Friendly, clear identity-document notice explains conditional non-Christian attendee submission and how to avoid entry problems, subject to legal review. Exact Arabic wording, English support, date/number formats, official brand files and customer notices require owner approval. Avoid exposing sensitive data on screen or in browser history.
