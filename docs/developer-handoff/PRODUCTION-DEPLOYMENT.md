# Future production deployment

Production deployment is explicitly not authorized by the Master Build Prompt. Do not buy hosting/domain, create provider accounts, connect payment/messaging, or publish the site.

Before a future launch: complete the Egyptian legal/privacy review; reconcile and sign off the canonical seat inventory; decide budget/provider and data region; secure Auth/session design and private storage; obtain production owner content; test backups/restore and incident handling; train staff; confirm network/scanner behavior; and obtain separate deployment authorization.

The USD 100/year target is an owner constraint, not evidence of feasibility. Public price comparison in `docs/pre-production/20-hosting-and-service-cost-comparison.md` found no complete safe/practical setup verified within that cap. Provider prices and terms must be rechecked before selection. Do not use Vercel Hobby for business production, or present a small unmanaged VM as a supportable complete solution.
